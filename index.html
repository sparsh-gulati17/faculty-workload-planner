Semester Status & Workflow

Academic workflow: Select a semester above, register its courses, add or update faculty profiles with eligible courses, then open Allocation to assign available 50-minute weekly slots.

Scheduling assumptions: One student group per semester, concurrent semester timetables across semesters, and no compulsory lunch break (Monday to Saturday, 9 daily slots = 54 weekly slots).

Courses

Each course belongs to a specific semester. Combined weekly classes in one semester cannot exceed 54 slots.
